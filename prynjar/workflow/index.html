PRynjar / Walkthrough

WASD. Space.
Your next review.

Move through a change with the familiar feel of FPS controls. Read at your pace, find the context you need, and turn what you notice into a review.

Swipe the images to read the interface, or open them at full size.

FPS controlsOpenReadFindDraftScale

01 / Get into the flow

FPS controls.
Your reading pace.

W and S move through the code. A and D move between files. Press Space to mark a file viewed and advance. FPS is the default, ready for your first review.

Tap again to accelerate, hold to maintain your pace, and pause to return to a walk. Adjust the reading speed with Shift + V. At the end of a file, continue into the next.

02 / Start with a repository

Review before you push.

Open a folder from disk with Shift + G, compare branches or commits, or review your uncommitted work.

Give each repository its own window, and return to recent work from the repositories view.

See the repositories view
See the changed-file list

03 / Read the code

The diff, with its context.

Read highlighted code with additions, deletions and line numbers in view. Press M for side by side, or E in FPS to open the whole file at the change.

Use the whole-file gutter to find other changed regions. Move between changes with Shift + W or S.

04 / Find what matters

Search the change.
Follow a line back.

Search file names, diff text, or the repository, with literal and regular-expression modes. Go straight to the code you want to inspect.

Press Y to explore a line’s history before the change and see who last touched it.

05 / Say what you found

Catch the thought.
Keep the flow.

Press C to anchor a comment to a selection on either side of the diff. Keep your drafts across sessions, and return to them when you’re ready.

Connect GitHub or GitLab to follow conversations and submit your comments as a complete review. Use the scoreboard to see what you’ve read and what remains.

06 / The larger view

Hundreds of files.
A clear path through.

Bring the whole change into one review. Filter by area, fold generated files and choose a reading order to find your way through a large update.

Mark files as you go and track what remains. Your progress stays with the review when you come back.
